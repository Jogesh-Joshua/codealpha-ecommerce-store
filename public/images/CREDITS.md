## 3. Specifications and Technical Compliance

- **Dimensions:** 1024×1024 px 1:1 square for all catalog products; 800×600 px 4:3 for homepage hero.
- **Format:** Modern WebP format with `-quality 85` compression, delivering lightweight payloads (13 KB – 221 KB) with sub-second network transfer.
- **Aesthetic:** Neutral warm-white and light-grey studio backgrounds, natural ambient shadows, clear material textures (wool felt, anodized aluminum, walnut grain, powder-coated steel, silicone).
- **Cleanliness:** Zero visible watermarks, no artificial text overlays, no intrusive brand logos, and zero collage layouts.
